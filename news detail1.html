Spain won the fifa world cup

Spain has won the men's FIFA World Cup two times, claiming the championship in 2010 and 2026.Their first title came in 2010 when they defeated the Netherlands 1-0 in South Africa. Their second and most recent victory was in July 2026, where they secured a 1-0 win against Argentina in extra time in the final in New Jersey, United States.
